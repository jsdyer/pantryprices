    Sources & method

    Each comparison uses one source series and the package sizes it published. The two monthly tables overlap in 2017–2022 and use different methods. Changing products, brands and retailers can affect averages. Use the Consumer Price Index for a measure of inflation.

    Annual urban grocery prices cover 1914–1959 and 1960–1970 in two publications. The Year Book supplies two 1985 benchmarks: eggs and partly skimmed milk. The other years from 1971 to 1994 are unreported here. Monthly prices begin in January 1995.
